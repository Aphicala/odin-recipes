Spaghetti Aglio Olio

Aglio is a very simple pasta dish, requiring minimal ingrediants and time taken to prepare and cook

I particularly enjoy it because of it's heavy use of garlic and the salty flavourbomb

The common ingredients to Spaghetti Aglio Olio are the following:

  • 1 pound uncooked spaghetti
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes, or to taste
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh italian parsley
  • 1 cup finely grated parmigiano-reggiano cheese
