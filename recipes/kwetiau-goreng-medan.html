Kwetiau Goreng Medan

Kwetiau Goreng Medan is a variant of Kwetiau Goreng that comes from the Chinese-Indonesian ethnic majority city of Medan.

They focus on more salty flavours, the following are ingredients commonly found:

  • Rice Noodle
  • Eggs
  • Wijen cooking oil
  • Micin
  • Soy Sauce
  • Bok Choy
  • Green Onions
  • Garlic
  • Shallots
  • Beansprouts
